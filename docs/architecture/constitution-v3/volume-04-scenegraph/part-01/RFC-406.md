# RFC-406 — Future Migration

Future DAOS development SHALL migrate every subsystem toward native SceneGraph usage.

Migration order:

```text
DecisionGraph

↓

SceneGraph

↓

Overlay

↓

Governance

↓

Benchmark

↓

Learning

↓

Providers

↓

Legacy removal
```

Backward compatibility adapters may exist during migration.

Adapters must never become permanent architecture.
