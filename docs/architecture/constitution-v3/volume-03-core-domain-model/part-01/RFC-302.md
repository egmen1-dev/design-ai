# RFC-302 — Commercial Genome

Commercial Genome is the complete commercial knowledge graph used by Decision Engine.

Commercial Genome consists of:

```text
Commercial Laws

↓

Commercial Rules

↓

Category Rules

↓

Brand DNA

↓

Adaptive Parameters

↓

Knowledge Rules

↓

Research Evidence
```

Commercial Genome is the primary knowledge source for commercial reasoning.
