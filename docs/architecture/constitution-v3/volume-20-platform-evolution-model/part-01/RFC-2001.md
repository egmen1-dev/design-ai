# RFC-2001 — Canonical Evolution Pipeline

Every change SHALL progress through the following lifecycle.

```text
Idea

↓

Research

↓

Experiment

↓

Evidence

↓

Knowledge Candidate

↓

ADR

↓

RFC

↓

Implementation

↓

Benchmark

↓

Certification

↓

Production

↓

Learning

↓

Commercial Genome
```

Skipping stages is prohibited.
