# SPEC-2201 — Lifecycle Transition Graph

Allowed transitions:

```text
idea
  ↓
hypothesis
  ↓
experimental
  ↓
candidate
  ↓
validated
  ↓
certified
  ↓
stable
  ↓
deprecated
  ↓
archived
```

Allowed rollback transitions:

```text
stable → certified
certified → validated
validated → candidate
candidate → experimental
```

Direct transition from `idea` to `certified` is prohibited.

Direct transition from `experimental` to `production` is prohibited.
