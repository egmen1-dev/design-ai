# DAOS Evolution Model

## Purpose

The Constitution defines the laws.

The Organization Model defines ownership.

The Cognitive Architecture defines thinking.

The Runtime Specification defines execution.

This Engineering Framework document defines how DAOS becomes better over time without losing architectural integrity.

This document SHALL become the canonical evolution model of the platform.

## Core Principle

DAOS SHALL NOT evolve by random feature addition.

DAOS SHALL evolve only through evidence.

Every improvement MUST originate from measurable observations.

No architectural component may evolve without governance.

Evolution is a controlled process.

## Sources of Evolution

Every change MUST originate from exactly one source.

Allowed sources:

- Research Evidence
- Benchmark Evidence
- Production Diagnostics
- Architecture Compliance Review
- Architecture Council Decision

No other source may introduce architectural change.

## Evolution Pipeline

Every improvement SHALL follow the same lifecycle.

No stage may be skipped.

## Types of Evolution

The platform evolves on multiple independent levels.

### Commercial Evolution

Improves commercial reasoning.

Examples:

- better Hero selection
- stronger visual hierarchy
- improved CTA placement
- better competitor differentiation

Output:

- Commercial Genome updates.

### Visual Evolution

Improves visual execution.

Examples:

- composition
- spacing
- lighting
- typography
- readability

Output:

- SceneGraph improvements.

### Knowledge Evolution

Improves what DAOS knows.

Output:

- Commercial Genome.
- Research Memory.
- Knowledge Base.

### Runtime Evolution

Improves execution efficiency and runtime intelligence.

Examples:

- better orchestration
- lower latency
- deterministic execution
- diagnostics quality

Output:

- Runtime improvements.

### Engineering Evolution

Improves maintainability and operational clarity.

Examples:

- ownership clarification
- simplification of execution boundaries
- improved determinism and traceability

Output:

- Architecture improvements.

## Evolution Rules (Proposed Improvement Contract)

Every proposed improvement SHALL define:

- Origin
- Objective
- Evidence
- Expected Benefit
- Risk
- Rollback
- Success Metric
- Owner

No anonymous improvements are allowed.

## Promotion Rules

A Knowledge Candidate SHALL NOT become Commercial Genome automatically.

Promotion requires:

- Evidence
- Benchmark
- Review
- Council Approval

Only after approval may Runtime consume new knowledge.

## Regression Protection

Every evolution SHALL verify:

- Commercial Quality
- Architecture Health
- Compliance
- Performance
- Determinism
- Maintainability

If any category regresses, promotion SHALL be rejected.

## Architectural Health Guarantees

Every evolution SHALL improve at least one of:

- Commercial Quality
- Knowledge Quality
- Architecture Health
- Research Capability
- Runtime Determinism
- Engineering Simplicity

Changes without measurable improvement SHALL NOT be promoted.

## Continuous Learning Separation

Learning SHALL NOT directly modify production state.

Learning creates proposals and confidence adjustments only.

Research validates proposals into governed knowledge candidates.

Council approves proposals.

Runtime consumes approved knowledge.

This separation SHALL NEVER be violated.

## Evolution Metrics (Owned and Governed)

The platform SHALL continuously measure:

- Architecture Health
- Compliance Score
- Commercial Score
- Knowledge Coverage
- Research Throughput
- Benchmark Stability
- Decision Explainability
- Prediction Accuracy
- Technical Debt (measured as governance-accountable operational cost)

Each metric SHALL have one owner.

## Evolution Anti-Patterns

The following are prohibited:

- Feature-first development
- Opinion-based improvements
- Silent runtime mutations
- Hidden retries
- Knowledge without evidence
- Evidence without benchmark
- Benchmark without diagnostics
- Research without hypothesis
- Genome mutation without approval
- Architecture changes without Council review

DAOS does not become better because developers add features.

DAOS becomes better because evidence improves decisions.

Every architectural change.

Every runtime change.

Every knowledge change.

Every research result.

Every benchmark.

## Final Principle

The purpose of DAOS is not to generate better images.

The purpose of DAOS is to continuously improve its commercial intelligence.

Images are temporary artifacts.

Commercial intelligence is the permanent asset.

## Acceptance Criteria

The document is accepted only if:

- every improvement follows a deterministic lifecycle;
- every evolution has measurable evidence;
- no production knowledge mutates automatically;
- governance remains the only promotion mechanism;
- the model is compatible with the Constitution, Runtime Specification, Cognitive Architecture, and Organization Model.
