# SPEC-205 — Experiment Series

Experiments rarely exist in isolation.

Related experiments form ExperimentSeries.

```yaml
ExperimentSeries:

  id:

  title:

  experiments:

  conclusions:

  promotedRules:
```

## Example

```text
Hero Design Research

↓

Experiment 01

↓

Experiment 02

↓

Experiment 03

↓

Commercial Conclusion
```
