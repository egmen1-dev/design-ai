# RFC-3001 — Research Pipeline

Every research initiative SHALL follow the canonical lifecycle.

```text
Observation

↓

Question

↓

Hypothesis

↓

Experiment Design

↓

Execution

↓

Evidence Collection

↓

Replication

↓

Cross-category Validation

↓

Knowledge Candidate

↓

Benchmark Validation

↓

Commercial Genome Promotion
```

Skipping stages is prohibited.
