# RFC-1101 — Canonical States

The generation lifecycle SHALL consist of the following canonical states.

```text
INITIALIZED

↓

RESEARCH_READY

↓

KNOWLEDGE_READY

↓

DECISION_READY

↓

DECISION_FINALIZED

↓

SCENE_READY

↓

EXECUTION_READY

↓

COMPOSITING

↓

OVERLAY_RENDERING

↓

RENDER_COMPLETED

↓

GOVERNANCE_RUNNING

↓

BENCHMARK_RUNNING

↓

LEARNING_RUNNING

↓

COMPLETED
```

No intermediate undocumented states SHALL exist.
